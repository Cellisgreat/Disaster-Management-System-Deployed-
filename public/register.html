CivicShield Disaster Management System

Report an emergency in your area

A citizen account lets you submit disaster requests — rescue, medical help or food — and follow how many people have been covered by the volunteers dispatched to you.

  • Submit requests with location and headcount
  • Track coverage progress live
  • Find the shortest route between city areas
  • Your requests stay private to your account
Self-registration always creates a PERSON account. Additional administrator accounts can only be created from inside the admin console.
